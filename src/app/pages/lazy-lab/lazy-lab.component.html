Lazy Loading

Lazy loading means JS is downloaded only when needed — not in the initial bundle. This page shows two flavors: route-level (loadComponent) and on-demand import when you click a button. Each card pairs Template + TypeScript.

1. Route lazy load (you’re here)

This page instance was created at {{ loadedAt }}. Navigating to /routes/lazy ran loadComponent and fetched this route’s chunk. Reload on Home, open DevTools → Network, then come back here to see that chunk.

  • Eager — component: SomeComponent (imported at the top of app.routes.ts)
  • Lazy — loadComponent: () => import(...).then(...) (dynamic import)
